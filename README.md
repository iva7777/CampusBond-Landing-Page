# CampusBond Landing Page 🚀

The official landing page for **CampusBond** – Your Digital Campus app. Built with modern web technologies, this responsive landing page showcases the application's features, student testimonials, and download options to encourage user acquisition.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [campusbond.vercel.app](https://campusbond.vercel.app)

---

## 📱 About CampusBond

CampusBond is a mobile application designed to help students balance their university life. It acts as the social heart of the campus, allowing students to meet new peers, discover events, manage academic deadlines, and trade essentials through a dedicated marketplace.

---

## ✨ Features Showcased

The landing page outlines 6 core pillars of the application:
1. **Event Hub:** Keep track of upcoming campus activities, parties, and lectures.
2. **Study Materials & Collaboration:** Search study groups, share lecture notes, and collaborate on projects.
3. **Peer Networking:** Find students from the same major, faculty, or with shared hobbies.
4. **Local Feed:** Ask questions, seek recommendations, and chat with the community.
5. **Deadline Reminders:** Never miss an exam, assignment, or university deadline.
6. **Student Marketplace:** A safe peer-to-peer market to buy/sell textbooks, dorm items, and services.

Follow these steps to set up the project locally.

Make sure you have Node.js installed (v18.x or higher recommended).
